Practice · Codecademy

Biodiversity in National Parks

A National Park Service analysis of species conservation status across four national parks. The dataset came from a Codecademy project, but I took the work further on my own.

Here's what it found. Out of 5,824 records and 5,541 unique species, only 179 species, or 3.23%, fell into a protected status. The other 96.8% were listed as “No Intervention”. A chi-square test between park and conservation status gave a chi² of 591.15 with p below 0.0001. But 23 of the 35 expected cells had counts under 5, so the chi-square assumptions don't hold. I reran it as a permutation test with 10,000 rounds, and the result stayed below p 0.0001. So the spread of conservation status does differ between parks.

The limitation: the data is very unbalanced, with almost everything sitting in one category. So this describes a pattern inside the dataset, not the real condition of the parks.

Python · Jupyter · pandas
