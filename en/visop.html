What VISOP is

VISOP, short for Visual Integrated Standard Operations Portal, is a web application for learning SOPs, the work procedures that employees have to follow. It is built with Google Apps Script and uses Google Sheets as its database. Because it runs on the web, it can be opened without installing anything. The content is arranged so newcomers can pick up the flow on their own without waiting to have it explained again and again.

My role

I worked on this project alone, with no team. From shaping the idea and designing the interface to building the app. Because I did it alone, I was also the one who set the order of the work.

Challenges and solutions

Two parts were the hardest: plenty of intricate code to work through, and page layouts I struggled to build the way I had pictured them. The submission deadline was tight as well, while my working hours meant I could not focus on building the app.

I worked around it by using my days off to look for examples and fix the code, including asking questions through Google. In the end I managed to resolve all of those problems.

Outcome

VISOP reached the semifinals of XXI Innovation Day 2026, an internal competition at Cinema XXI. At that stage the project was judged directly by the Judging Board and Executive Management at Cinema XXI.
