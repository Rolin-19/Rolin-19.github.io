01 About

Yogyakarta, Indonesia.

I started working in 2021, while studying for a D1 Ground Handling diploma at Sekolah Tinggi Teknologi Kedirgantaraan. There I got used to strict rules on timing and safety. I interned in passenger services at PT Gapura Angkasa, then worked as a train attendant at PT Reska Multi Usaha.

In retail I worked directly with numbers. As Assistant Chief of Store at Lawson Indonesia, I built shift schedules for seven crew members and wrote daily sales reports. Those reports were used to decide which products needed restocking. I carried that record-keeping habit to Miniso and Cinema XXI.

Now I focus on data analysis. I completed Python and SQL courses, then worked on practice projects: a conservation data analysis of four national parks, a movie recommendation program, and a food recommendation finder. I document everything so it can be reviewed.
